# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release, for game version 3.290.1 and ZHMModSDK 4.2.1.

- Tracks the debris around 47 and 47 himself, with real boxes for the pieces near him.
- Violins and infinite ammo for testing; removal of all the debris around 47.
- Measuring tools: timers, PhysX state, piece description, collide check, set-down probe, ray from his feet,
  rocket log, boost recorder and the launch by hand.
